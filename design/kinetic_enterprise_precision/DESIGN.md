## Brand & Style

This design system defines an authoritative, editorial, high-octane visual identity tailored for modern enterprise technology learning. Merging the raw visual impact of high-contrast contemporary studio portfolios with technical rigor, it transforms enterprise software training (SAP) into an aspirational, elite career accelerator.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Unapologetic Authority:** Powerful condensed typography communicates industrial scale, corporate weight, and executive readiness.
- **Surgical Precision:** Strict hairline structures, razor-thin borders, and calibrated mono-styled section trackers emphasize operational clarity.
- **Architectural Minimalism:** Expansive neutral stone space accented by high-voltage amber and cyan focal points avoids typical ed-tech clutter and evokes high-end product engineering studios.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
The visual style draws from **Modern Swiss Editorial Brutalism fused with Tactile Studio Minimalism**:
- Monolithic, tightly tracked uppercase headlines juxtaposed against neutral, expressive geometric body copy.
- Structural modularity (bento-grid architecture) relying on clean hairline dividers, generous contour corners, and strict compositional tension.
- Tactile, crisp, daylight lighting (cool concrete and industrial paper tones) punctuated by high-contrast blocks and electric amber/cyan focal points.

## Colors

The system uses a strictly disciplined, reductionist chromatic strategy built around vibrant amber (`#E77801`), grounded secondary tones (`#A26A40`), and a sharp cyan tertiary accent (`#00B5F5`). Backgrounds rely on architectural stone values rather than sterile whites, delivering depth and natural optical focus.

### Surface & Atmosphere Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#84746a`):** The primary physical substrate across pages, establishing a tangible architectural mood.
- **Canvas Atmospheric Glow (`#F0F1F3`):** Utilized exclusively as ultra-soft radial highlights to create tactile studio illumination.
- **Module White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority modular cards, bento modules, and interactive controls.
- **Neutral Accent Surface (`#D3D6DA`):** Mid-tone module containers, nested metric badges, and secondary card footers.
- **Monolith Dark (`#0A0A0A`):** Pure black surfaces used for high-impact module contrast panels, executive summary cards, and footer blocks.

### Ink & State Rules
- **Ink Primary (`#0A0A0A`):** Strict high-contrast text rendering on all light surfaces.
- **Ink Secondary (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.55)`):** Metadata, module labels, course durations, and technical specs.
- **Accent Primary (`#E77801`):** Reserved strictly for primary intent actions, interactive focus rings, active module steps, and directional markers.
- **Separators (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`):** Surgical 1px structural hairline rules.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial dynamic between raw visual weight and structural neutrality.

### Headline Archetype (`Anton`)
- Display and headline levels are set in uppercase, condensed grotesque lettering.
- Leading is tightly bounded (1:1 ratio on massive scales) to enforce architectural block density.
- Headlines command immediate focus, acting as structural graphic elements within the grid rather than traditional running headers.

### Body Archetype (`Bricolage Grotesque`)
- Bricolage Grotesque handles all informative, instructional, and narrative content with a distinct contemporary edge.
- Expressive structural curves ensure effortless readability and character when presenting complex enterprise syllabi, ERP certification pathways, and technical course milestones.

### System Labeling & Section Indexing
- Category and navigation tags strictly feature the distinctive **Dot Marker Convention** (`• COURSES`, `• ARCHITECTURE`, `• METHODOLOGY`).
- Always styled in uppercase using `label-mono`, set with positive letter spacing (`0.08em`), with the dot colored in Primary Amber (`#E77801`) to produce an editorial index effect.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a modern asymmetric 12-column bento-grid structure.

### Grid & Page Structure
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max inner content container width is capped at 1440px for precise editorial cadence.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Horizontal panels preserve aspect ratios while stack orders prioritize lead metrics.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Bento card rows reflow into sequential vertical cards with touch targets padded to minimum 48px height.

### Spacing Rhythm & Editorial Density
- Internal component padding is generous (`space-xl` / 40px) inside cards to create breathing space against the dense display typography.
- Section boundaries employ high vertical space (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish distinct modular chapters throughout the page.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects muddy drop shadows and heavy blur stacks, relying instead on structural plane changes and crisp, physical boundaries.

### Flat Tactile Layering
Depth is produced through the calibrated layering of planar surfaces:
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Stone substrate illuminated with broad, diffuse radial highlights.
2. **Level 1 (Module Layer):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` and `#D3D6DA` panels floating directly on the canvas, bounded exclusively by 1px hairline strokes (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`).
3. **Level 2 (Inverted Monoliths):** Pure `#0A0A0A` contrasting panels that introduce dramatic tonal shifts and visual anchors.
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Active States):** Hovered cards gain an imperceptible physical lift via a microscopic razor shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.06)`, maintaining crisp studio rigor.

## Shapes

The geometry creates a balanced visual rhythm by combining crisp, modern structural radii with streamlined pill shapes for all interactive elements.

### Structural Radius Standards
- **Containers & Bento Cards:** Refined `8px` to `12px` radius across all module tiles, carousel panels, and horizontal feature blocks, favoring precise technical framing.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons & Filter Chips):** Full pill curvature (`9999px`), creating immediate visual separation between informational containers and clickable triggers.
- **Form Inputs & Inner Badges:** `6px` radius for focused input fields and embedded micro-stat tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill Solid):** Background `#E77801`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Inter` 14px Weight 600, padding `16px 36px`, border-radius `9999px`. Subtle scale transition (`scale(1.02)`) and brightness boost on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Ghost Inverted):** Background transparent, 1px solid `#0A0A0A`, text `#0A0A0A`, font `Inter` 14px Weight 600, border-radius `9999px`, padding `16px 32px`. Transitions to solid `#0A0A0A` with `#FFFFFF` text on hover.
- **Dark Surface CTA:** When placed on Monolith Dark (`#0A0A0A`) surfaces, buttons utilize pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with pure black text (`#0A0A0A`).

### Cards & Bento Panels
- **Standard Module:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 12px border radius, 1px hairline border `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`, internal padding `40px`.
- **Feature Monolith Module:** Background `#0A0A0A`, text `#FFFFFF`, 12px border radius, internal padding `40px`. Sub-elements use hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Inner Dividers:** Any segmented section inside a card must use a horizontal or vertical 1px line in `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`.

### Chips & Filter Indicators
- **Category Dot Chips:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `9999px`, padding `8px 16px`, hairline border `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`. Text format: `• SAP S/4HANA`, where the dot is a styled span colored in `#E77801`.
- **Active State:** Background `#0A0A0A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border color `#0A0A0A`, with the dot remaining `#E77801`.

### Input Fields
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 6px border radius, 1px solid `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.12)`, padding `16px 20px`, text `#0A0A0A`, placeholder `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.4)`. Focus state produces a crisp `0 0 0 2px #E77801` ring without soft ambient glow.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px × 20px`, 4px border radius for checkboxes, pill for radios. Unchecked state: hairline border `rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.2)`. Checked state: solid `#E77801` fill with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` checkmark icon.

### Technical Curriculum List
- Monospaced numeric indexing (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in `Inter` 12px Weight 600 alongside high-impact bold titles.
- Rows separated by edge-to-edge 1px hairline dividers (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.08)`).
- Hovering over a row highlights the trailing link arrow in `#E77801` with an ease-out horizontal translation of `4px`.
